Making Your Study Abroad Journey More Affordable

At Welhan Abroad Study Consultancy, we understand that pursuing higher education overseas can be an exciting yet financially challenging journey. To make things easier for our students, we are delighted to introduce our Student Discount Program, designed to provide additional support and encouragement as you take the next big step toward your future.

By filling out this Student Discount Application Form, you will have the opportunity to enjoy exclusive benefits, reduced service fees, and special offers on selected programs. Our goal is to ensure that deserving students can access high-quality consultancy services without worrying too much about costs.

This discount is available for students enrolling in our test preparation courses (IELTS, PTE, TOEFL), as well as those seeking our counseling and application services for universities abroad. We believe that every hardworking student deserves the chance to turn their international education dream into reality, and financial limitations should not stand in the way.

Simply complete the form with your details, and our team will review your application promptly. Together, let’s make your study abroad plan not just a dream, but an achievable success—at a cost that fits your budget.
