🌟 Skill Assessment Support at Welhan Abroad Study

At Welhan Abroad Study, we provide end-to-end, personalized assistance to make your Skill Assessment process seamless and successful.

🔎 Identifying the Right Assessing Authority

Choosing the correct assessing body is the first step. Each occupation has its own requirements, and we ensure you apply to the right authority:

  • ACS – IT Professionals
  • VETASSESS – General occupations & trades
  • Engineers Australia – Engineers (all disciplines)
  • ACECQA – Early Childhood Teacher
  • ANMAC – Nurses & Midwives
  • TRA – Trades Recognition Australia
  • AHPRA – Healthcare practitioners
  • CPA | CA ANZ | IPA – Accountants & Finance Specialists

📂 Reviewing & Preparing Documents

We guide you with a clear checklist and ensure every document meets Australian standards:

  • Academic certificates & transcripts
  • Employment reference letters
  • Updated professional CV/resume
  • English proficiency evidence (if required)
  • Supporting occupation-specific documents

📝 Step-by-Step Application Guidance

Our expert consultants assist you with:

  • Accurate form completion & formatting
  • Submitting complete and verifiable evidence
  • Meeting strict deadlines
  • Professional communication with assessing bodies

🎯 Pathways After Assessment

Once you receive a positive skills assessment, we help you plan the next steps:

  • Skilled Migration visas (subclass 189, 190, 491)
  • Employer-Sponsored visas (subclass 482, 186)
  • Post-study graduate visa pathways
  • Career development & further study opportunities

Connecting students to world-class universities with reliable guidance.
