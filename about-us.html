Welhan Abroad Study – Your Gateway to Global Education!

At Welhan Abroad Study, we turn your aspirations into achievements with the support of our trusted, experienced study abroad consultants. We understand that studying abroad can be complex, which is why we provide tailored guidance—from selecting the right course and university to securing your student visa.

  • Access a global network of prestigious and accredited universities
  • Get expert insights from our certified and experienced consultants
  • Benefit from one-on-one support tailored to your academic goals
  • Navigate your study abroad journey with confidence and clarity

Guiding Your Global Journey

Personalized support for study abroad success.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eventually transform the abroad study experience for students from Nepal to bridge the skills gap in this global age so that they get a fair shot at every opportunity to transform the world in their own way. We are proud to help students confidently choosing the right program for their study & career and also help them with their documentation process to their chosen destinations namely Canada, Australia, South Korea, United Kingdom, United States, China, Japan, New Zealand, Germany, etc.

Our Vision

We are currently developing a user-friendly platform backed up by the latest cutting-edge Artificial intelligence algorithm, one that is specifically designed for students considering abroad studies. We expect it will be a great asset whether students are looking for the best program or lodging a well-packaged visa application. All of that through a single platform from the comfort of their homes without the need to even visit the office in person.

Our Infrastructure

The main aim of establishing the company is to help the students to get the student visa of their chosen country. During this Welhan Team will be helping to make a university application to visa application including the supporting documentation which helps to convert visa application into a visa success story.

Connecting students to world-class universities with reliable guidance.
